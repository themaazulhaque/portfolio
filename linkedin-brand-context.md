## Writing Voice

**Tone:** Direct, humble, technically credible, slightly conversational

**Style:**
- Short sentences and paragraphs
- Concrete details over abstract claims
- First person, not third person
- Conversational but not casual
- Technical but accessible
- Honest about failures and learning

**Voice markers:**
- "I built..."
- "I learned..."
- "Here's what broke..."
- "The fix was..."
- "What I'd do differently..."

**Never use:**
- "I am thrilled to announce"
- "Leveraging cutting-edge technology"
- "Game-changing"
- "Unlocking potential"
- "In today's fast-paced world"
- "Passionate about"
- "Driven to"
- "Results-oriented"
- "Thought leader"
- "Guru"
- "Ninja"
- "Rockstar"

## Preferred CTA Style

**Good CTAs:**
- "What's your experience with X?"
- "Have you hit this issue? How did you fix it?"
- "What would you do differently?"
- "Share your approach in the comments"

**Bad CTAs:**
- "Like if you agree!"
- "Tag someone who needs this"
- "Comment X to get Y"
- "Follow for more"

---

## Preferred Post Structure

1. **Hook** (first 210 chars): Specific, attention-grabbing, not a question
2. **Context** (2-3 sentences): Set up the situation or problem
3. **Body** (3-5 short paragraphs): The actual content, lessons, or story
4. **Takeaway** (1-2 sentences): What you learned or what matters
5. **CTA** (1 sentence): Question or discussion prompt
6. **P.S.** (optional): Additional context or link

**Formatting:**
- 1-2 sentences per paragraph
- Double line-breaks between ideas
- Short paragraphs (mobile-first)
- No external links in body
- 0-2 hashtags at end
