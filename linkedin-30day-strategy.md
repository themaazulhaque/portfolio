# 30-Day LinkedIn Content Strategy

> A complete 30-day plan for building a strong LinkedIn presence as a software engineer.
> Optimized for credibility, conversations, and recruiter/developer visibility.

---

## Strategy Overview

**Goal:** Build a strong, authentic LinkedIn presence as a software engineer who builds things, learns quickly, shares useful technical knowledge, and documents real progress.

**Target outcomes:**
- Profile visits from recruiters and hiring managers
- Connections with other developers and engineers
- Comments and conversations on technical posts
- Recognition as someone who ships real projects
- Organic reach to target audience

**NOT the goal:**
- Viral fame
- Fake authority
- Motivational speaker persona
- AI-influencer aesthetic

---

## Weekly Cadence

### Posting Schedule
- **Monday**: Technical insight post (Developer Learning or Practical Education)
- **Wednesday**: Build-in-public / project story (Building in Public)
- **Friday**: Career or personal lesson (Career Journey)
- **Tuesday/Thursday**: Comments and networking only
- **Weekend**: Off or light engagement

### Commenting Strategy
- 3-5 substantive comments per active LinkedIn day
- Focus on posts from target creators (developers, CTOs, hiring managers)
- Add value, don't self-promote
- Be among first commenters on viral posts

## Week 1: Foundation (Days 1-7)

### Day 1 (Monday) - Technical Insight
**Pillar:** Developer Learning
**Hook formula:** F7 (Odd-Precision Money Ledger)
**Topic:** "The one React pattern that saved me 4 hours of debugging"
**Angle:** Share a specific pattern you learned, with a concrete example
**CTA:** "What's your go-to debugging pattern?"

### Day 2 (Tuesday) - Comments Only
**Action:** Comment on 3-5 posts from React/Next.js developers
**Pattern:** T1 (Missing-Piece) or T4 (Practitioner Observation)
**Target:** Developers with 1k-10k followers in your niche

### Day 3 (Wednesday) - Build in Public
**Pillar:** Building in Public
**Hook formula:** F5 (Self-Proving Meta)
**Topic:** "I just shipped [project name]. Here's what broke."
**Angle:** Real story about building something, what went wrong, how you fixed it
**CTA:** "What's the last bug that took you longer than expected?"

### Day 4 (Thursday) - Comments Only
**Action:** Comment on 3-5 posts from CTOs or engineering managers
**Pattern:** T2 (Answer-the-Closing-Question) or T3 (Data-First)
**Target:** People who make hiring decisions

### Day 5 (Friday) - Career Lesson
**Pillar:** Career Journey
**Hook formula:** F3 (Year-over-Year Pivot)
**Topic:** "3 things I learned during my first internship"
**Angle:** Honest lessons from real experience, not generic advice
**CTA:** "What was your biggest internship lesson?"

### Day 6-7 (Weekend) - Light Engagement
**Action:** Like and comment on 2-3 posts
**Pattern:** Short, valuable comments only

## Week 2: Building Authority (Days 8-14)

### Day 8 (Monday) - Technical Education
**Pillar:** Practical Technical Education
**Hook formula:** F15 (Explain-to-Kids)
**Topic:** "React Server Components explained simply"
**Angle:** Break down a complex concept into something anyone can understand
**CTA:** "What concept do you want explained simply?"

### Day 9 (Tuesday) - Comments Only
**Action:** Comment on 3-5 posts
**Pattern:** Mix of T1-T4
**Target:** Python/Django/FastAPI developers

### Day 10 (Wednesday) - Build in Public
**Pillar:** Building in Public
**Hook formula:** F7 (Odd-Precision Money Ledger)
**Topic:** "My [project] costs $X/month to run. Here's the breakdown."
**Angle:** Real costs, real numbers, real decisions
**CTA:** "What surprised you most about your infrastructure costs?"

### Day 11 (Thursday) - Comments Only
**Action:** Comment on 3-5 posts
**Pattern:** T5 (Counter-with-Concession) on a controversial take
**Target:** People discussing architecture or tech choices

### Day 12 (Friday) - AI + Product
**Pillar:** AI + Product Engineering
**Hook formula:** F10 (Contrarian + Historical Receipts)
**Topic:** "AI won't replace developers. Here's what it will do."
**Angle:** Contrarian take backed by real experience building AI apps
**CTA:** "What's your experience with AI in development?"

### Day 13-14 (Weekend) - Light Engagement
**Action:** Share or comment on 2-3 relevant posts

## Week 3: Deepening Connections (Days 15-21)

### Day 15 (Monday) - Technical Deep Dive
**Pillar:** Developer Learning
**Hook formula:** F17 (Controlled A/B Anecdote)
**Topic:** "I tried [tool A] vs [tool B] for [use case]. Here's what happened."
**Angle:** Honest comparison with real results
**CTA:** "Which do you prefer for [use case]?"

### Day 16 (Tuesday) - Comments Only
**Action:** Comment on 3-5 posts
**Pattern:** T6 (Quotable-Reframe) on a popular post
**Target:** React/Next.js community leaders

### Day 17 (Wednesday) - Build in Public
**Pillar:** Building in Public
**Hook formula:** F11 (Emotional Cold-Open)
**Topic:** "The moment I almost gave up on [project]"
**Angle:** Real struggle, real perseverance, real outcome
**CTA:** "What's keeping you going on your current project?"

### Day 18 (Thursday) - Comments Only
**Action:** Comment on 3-5 posts
**Pattern:** T7 (Ask-a-Sharper-Question)
**Target:** People discussing deployment or DevOps

### Day 19 (Friday) - Career Journey
**Pillar:** Career Journey
**Hook formula:** F4 (Time-Anchor Confession)
**Topic:** "I applied to 47 jobs before getting my first offer"
**Angle:** Honest job search story, what worked, what didn't
**CTA:** "How many applications did it take you?"

### Day 20-21 (Weekend) - Light Engagement
**Action:** Engage with 2-3 posts from your network

---

## Week 4: Consolidation (Days 22-30)

### Day 22 (Monday) - Technical Tip
**Pillar:** Practical Technical Education
**Hook formula:** F19 (Anecdote-Meets-Evidence Bridge)
**Topic:** "I kept making this MongoDB mistake. Here's the fix."
**Angle:** Personal mistake + data on why it matters
**CTA:** "What's a mistake you see junior devs make?"

### Day 23 (Tuesday) - Comments Only
**Action:** Comment on 3-5 posts
**Pattern:** Mix of templates
**Target:** People you've engaged with before (warm connections)

### Day 24 (Wednesday) - Build in Public
**Pillar:** Building in Public
**Hook formula:** F20 (Diverging-Curves Close)
**Topic:** "Month 1 vs Month 3 of building [project]"
**Angle:** Show real progress, real numbers, real learning
**CTA:** "What's the biggest thing you've learned this month?"

### Day 25 (Thursday) - Comments Only
**Action:** Comment on 3-5 posts
**Pattern:** T3 (Data-First) on a trending topic
**Target:** AI/ML developers and researchers

### Day 26 (Friday) - Month in Review
**Pillar:** Career Journey
**Hook formula:** F3 (Year-over-Year Pivot)
**Topic:** "What I learned building in public for 30 days"
**Angle:** Honest reflection on the experience
**CTA:** "What's your biggest lesson from this month?"

### Day 27-30 (Remaining days) - Light Engagement
**Action:** Engage with network, share relevant content
**Focus:** Strengthen connections made during the month

## Content Examples by Pillar

### Building in Public Examples
- "I just deployed my first Next.js app to production. Here's what I learned about Vercel."
- "My React Native app crashed 47 times in the first week. Here are the 3 bugs that caused it."
- "I spent 6 hours debugging a MongoDB query. The fix was one line."
- "Here's the architecture I chose for my SaaS app and why."

### Developer Learning Examples
- "React useEffect runs twice in development. Here's why and how to handle it."
- "The one Next.js pattern that makes API routes cleaner."
- "Why I switched from Express to FastAPI for my Python backend."
- "MongoDB indexes: the one thing that made my queries 10x faster."

### AI + Product Engineering Examples
- "I built an AI chatbot in 2 hours using LangChain. Here's the code."
- "The prompt that made my AI app actually useful."
- "Why most AI features fail: a developer's perspective."
- "How I integrated GPT-4 into my SaaS product."

### Career Journey Examples
- "My internship taught me more than any bootcamp."
- "The freelance mistake that cost me a client."
- "What I wish I knew before my first coding interview."
- "How I went from tutorial hell to building real projects."

### Practical Technical Education Examples
- "Git rebase vs merge: when to use which (simply explained)."
- "The debugging technique that saved me hours."
- "API design: the one rule that prevents 90% of bugs."
- "TypeScript tips: the 3 types I use every day."

## Measuring Success

### Weekly Metrics
- Profile views
- Post impressions
- Comments received
- Connection requests
- Inbound messages

### Monthly Metrics
- Follower growth
- Comment engagement rate
- Profile view trend
- Connection quality (recruiters, developers, CTOs)
- Content performance by pillar

### What "Success" Looks Like After 30 Days
- 50+ new relevant connections
- 10+ meaningful conversations started
- 5+ recruiter profile views
- Recognition in your niche (people start recognizing your name)
- A library of 12+ posts you're proud of

## Adjustments

After Week 1, review:
- Which posts got the most engagement?
- Which comments got author replies?
- What topics resonated most?
- What time slots worked best?

Adjust Weeks 2-4 based on data.
